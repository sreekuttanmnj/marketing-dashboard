Funnel snapshot (Mar 20 – May 20, OCW only): 41,163 signups → 448 KYC approved of 690 reviewed (64.9% KYC approval rate) / 459 KYB approved of 726 reviewed (63.2% KYB approval rate) → 673 new subscriptions. KYC and KYB run nearly in lockstep (most signups go through both together). Signup → Subscription = 1.63% is the real paid-conversion rate; Signup → KYC-approved = 1.09% is the verified-customer rate. Subscriptions exceed KYC/KYB approvals (673 vs ~450) — either some plans don't require KYC, KYC happens after subscribing, or there's a timing lag between subscription start and verification completion. Worth confirming with product.
GA4 events vs internal DB — both directions of error: GA4 live-sign-up shows 8,223 vs internal DB 41,163 signups (~5× under-fire — missing server-side / mobile / API signups). GA4 live-upgrade-plan shows 3,616 vs internal DB 616 subscriptions for W13–W20 (~6× over-fire — likely counting clicks/intent rather than completed payments). For headline reporting, use the Internal-DB columns. The GA4 event counts stay in the OCW Conversion Events table for tracking-health monitoring.

Notes & Data-Quality Caveats

Tracking gaps to be aware of

ZilMoney signup event is unreliable. The dedicated signup event (singup_zm) fires fewer than 1 time per week. The dashboard uses contact_button_click and generate_lead as lead proxies. Authoritative ZilMoney signup numbers should come from the product database.
OCW sessions are heavily weighted toward existing-user logins. The /login page accounts for ~50% of OCW sessions. "New acquisition" is better represented by the New Users column or the trial-signup event. When discussing acquisition, prefer those over raw Sessions.
Reporting window is Mar 20 – May 20, 2026. Source granularity limits exact trimming. The breakdown tables show 8 complete ISO weeks (W13–W20, Mar 23 – May 17), which sit entirely inside the window but exclude the 3 leading days (Mar 20–22, partial W12) and the 3 trailing days (May 18–20, partial W21). Internal-DB columns (Signups, KYC, KYB, Subscriptions) do have data for the W12 and W21 edge weeks — that data is included in the headline window totals (e.g., 41,163 signups, 673 subscriptions) but not the weekly breakdown rows below.
"Paid Search" in the GA4 channel-mix charts is a subset of total Ads activity. The Google Ads account ran $2,986.29 in spend across Search, Display, Video, Demand Gen, App, and Performance Max (see the Google Ads section). The "Paid Search" channel in the OCW/ZM channel-mix charts only reflects GA4's attribution of sessions to the paid-search channel, so it will not match the full Ads click total (4,743). For platform-level Ads numbers, refer to the Google Ads section, not the channel-mix charts.

Methodology

Reporting window is Mar 20 – May 20, 2026 (matching the Google Ads export window). Traffic data is pulled from the GA4 Data API with the country dimension filtered to "United States." Search data is pulled from the Google Search Console API with the country filter set to USA. Breakdown tables use ISO weeks (Monday start, Sunday end): 8 complete weeks W13–W20 (Mar 23 – May 17) — the largest set of complete weeks fitting inside the window. Internal-DB sources (signups, KYC, KYB, subscriptions) have full weekly coverage including partial edge weeks W12 and W21, which are summed into the headline window totals but not shown as separate breakdown rows. Both OnlineCheckWriter and ZilMoney report in America/Chicago, so periods line up cleanly across brands.
